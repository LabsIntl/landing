3. AI Voice Calling — Disclosure

IMPORTANT — FCC 2024 DECLARATORY RULING:

LABS International uses AI-generated voice technology (via Vapi) for certain outbound calls. Under the FCC's Declaratory Ruling of February 8, 2024, AI-generated voice calls are classified as "artificial voice" calls under 47 U.S.C. § 227 (the TCPA). These calls are legally equivalent to robocalls.

We will only place AI voice calls to individuals who have given prior express written consent or verifiable prior express consent. All AI voice calls will disclose at the start of the call: (1) the calling entity name (LABS International), (2) that the caller is an AI, and (3) the reason for the call.

How We Obtain Consent for AI Voice Calls

Our primary consent model is as follows: A representative of LABS International contacts you by phone (human-to-human call) to introduce our services. If you express interest and verbally agree to receive a follow-up AI demo call, that verbal consent is recorded in our consent log (timestamp, your name, number, and the representative who obtained consent). We may also send you an SMS asking you to confirm: "Reply YES to confirm you'd like LABS International's AI to call you for a 3-minute demo. Reply STOP to opt out at any time." Your YES reply constitutes prior express written consent under the TCPA.

Alternatively, you may provide consent via the web form at labsintl.com/opt-in, which constitutes Prior Express Written Consent (PEWC) under 47 C.F.R. § 64.1200.

4. SMS Messaging — A2P 10DLC Disclosure

A2P 10DLC Registration: LABS International's SMS messaging campaigns are registered under the CTIA/carrier A2P 10DLC program. Our messages are sent via SignalWire on registered 10-digit long code numbers.

Types of SMS We Send

  • Consent confirmation SMS: Sent after a verbal consent conversation to confirm opt-in ("Reply YES to confirm…")
  • Appointment reminders: Sent to contacts who have booked a demo or call
  • Follow-up messages: Sent only to opted-in contacts regarding AI automation services
  • Transactional messages: Confirmation messages, call summaries, next-step instructions

SMS Opt-Out Instructions

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. Upon receipt of a STOP message, we will immediately cease all SMS communications to that number and add it to our internal Do-Not-Contact list. You may also opt back in by replying START. For assistance at any time, reply HELP to any message from us, or email info@labsintl.com.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All opt-in and opt-out requests are honored immediately and are permanently logged.

Standard message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your engagement with our services.

5. Do-Not-Call & Do-Not-Contact

LABS International maintains an internal Do-Not-Contact (DNC) list. Any individual who requests to be removed from our calling or messaging list — verbally, via SMS STOP, or via written request — will be added to this list immediately and will not be contacted again by any automated or AI-assisted system.

We also comply with the Federal Trade Commission's National Do-Not-Call Registry for residential lines, and applicable state DNC registries. Our AI voice agent is programmed to recognize verbal opt-out phrases including: "remove me," "don't call," "stop calling," "take me off your list," and similar expressions. Upon recognition of any opt-out phrase, the call will end immediately and the number will be added to our internal DNC list.

To be added to our Do-Not-Contact list: Email info@labsintl.com with your phone number and "OPT OUT" in the subject line, reply STOP to any SMS, or say "remove me" or "stop calling" during any call from us.

6. Call Recording

Calls with our AI voice agent (and optionally human calls made by our representatives) may be recorded for quality assurance and training purposes. We comply with applicable state wiretapping and two-party consent laws. The following states require all-party consent for call recording: California, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Montana, Nevada, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania, Washington, and Connecticut.

If you are located in one of these states, you will be notified at the beginning of the call that it may be recorded. If you do not consent to recording, you may request that recording be disabled, or you may end the call. Continuing the call after the recording disclosure constitutes your consent to recording.
